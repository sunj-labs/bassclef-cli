# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want everyone to feel welcome in this project. Bassclef ships for
adopters at every level of experience — the person who found it in
a tweet, the solo operator between marketing cycles, the senior
engineer running an audit. All three should feel safe filing bugs,
asking questions, and pushing back on design.

## What we expect

- Be kind. Write your issue or PR the way you would want to receive one.
- Assume the other person is doing their best. Bugs are not accusations.
- Say what you mean. Hedge language slows everyone down.
- If you disagree with a decision, say so directly and explain why.
- If you make a mistake, say so. Nobody gets shipped a perfect substrate.

## What is not welcome

- Personal attacks on maintainers, contributors, or adopters.
- Harassment of any kind — in issues, PRs, discussions, or private messages.
- Sharing private information (email, address, employer) without consent.
- Sustained bad-faith argumentation. One or two hot messages we can work
  through. A pattern is different.

## Reporting

Send a private email to `maintainer@bassclef.dev`. Include:

- What happened.
- Where it happened (issue link, PR link, message).
- What outcome you want.

The maintainer will read the report within 72 hours and reply with the
next step. Reports are kept private by default.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may — at their discretion —

- Edit or remove the message.
- Ask the person to walk their message back or take it to email.
- Block the person from the repo, temporarily or permanently.

We prefer conversation over enforcement. Enforcement is a floor, not
a first move.

## Scope

This applies to all bassclef spaces — GitHub issues + PRs + discussions
on `sunj-labs/bassclef-cli`, `sunj-labs/bassclef`, and any other
sunj-labs repo where bassclef work happens. It applies in public and in
private one-on-one messages that reference bassclef work.
